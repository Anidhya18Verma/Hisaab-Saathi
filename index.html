    Questions owners ask

    Does this site file my returns?

    No. It tells you what applies to you and when, so you walk into the portal prepared. The actual filing happens on gst.gov.in for GST and incometax.gov.in for income tax.

    Do I still need a Chartered Accountant?

    Often not for a small, simple business. A CA is worth the fee when your turnover crosses the audit limit, when you get a notice, or when you are unsure which return form fits you.

    What is input tax credit?

    When you buy for your business, the supplier charges you GST. You can subtract that GST from the GST you collect on your sales, so you pay only the difference. The invoice at the top of this page does that sum for you.

    Are UPI and card sales counted as turnover?

    Yes. Every rupee of sales counts, whether it arrives as cash, UPI, card or bank transfer. UPI records make your books easier to prove, so keep the monthly statements.

    What happens if I miss a due date?

    Late fees and interest apply, and the amounts depend on the form. Repeated GST delays can also block your next return or your input credit. File late rather than not at all, then set a reminder for the next date.
